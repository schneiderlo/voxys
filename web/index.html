    How to build

    First, select Cargo cradle → Remove → Keep to clear deck space. Choose a brick once, then click repeatedly to build. Use Rotate to turn the preview. Green fits; red is blocked. Select parts stops; Undo reverses the last placement. Launch leaves out the unused preview.

    Select, copy & mirror

    Shift-click parts to select a group.

    Mirror copies symmetric bricks across the boat's X = 0 or Z = 0 plane. Move the preview until it fits, then Keep. Copying buys new parts at Launch.
