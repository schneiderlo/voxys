# Cove filtered-lighting and surface composition review

Reviewed the live Cove opt-in changes in `src/render/blit_path.hpp/.cpp`, `src/render/scene_shadows.hpp`, `shaders/ray_blit.wgsl`, and `shaders/water_clipmap.wgsl`, plus the fixture environment callback ordering. This is bounded rendering/source review and focused numeric evidence, not whole-game visual approval.

The separate Cove terrain entry consumes the actual MeshPath filtered specular/diffuse cubes and BRDF LUT. The unused sky LUT binding8 is absent only from the Cove terrain layout and entries, keeping the sampled fragment texture count at the supported16. Legacy entries/layouts retain their original bindings. The filter bake is encoded before the consumer callback, which constructs a candidate bind group atomically, caches identical source identities and retains the texture views through GPU bind-group ownership. The sampler clamps LUT edges. Move/shutdown/empty-owner paths transfer or clear these resources; raw cached identities are not independently sampled after clearing. No new lifetime or layout defect found.

Two concrete material issues were corrected under root authorization: Cove-only GGX now retains the normalized narrow peak using a1e-12 denominator floor (legacy1e-6 unchanged); wet terrain now uses the same bounded separate film/substrate equations as MeshPath. Neighbor normals provide bounded variance without illegal derivatives in a depth-conditional fragment path.

The first actual composition check then exposed a third issue: fully submerged receivers were adding an air/water film that the ocean compositor adds again. The corrected immersion channel and terrain waterline response remove this duplicate interface, while preserving water/substrate dielectric Fresnel, conductor identity, authored alpha and shadow/depth ownership. Cove water absorption and post-ACES presentation remain root's explicit opt-in changes, with no material lighting/exposure modifications to fit a pixel threshold. Both final direct-HDR Cove and original legacy cases passed in `checks/cove-blit-r05.test.xml`; source hashes and exact values are in its summary JSON.

Independent sibling review of the resulting immersion equations and fixture ownership found no further actionable issue. These checks do not establish final water/terrain art quality, all animation/LOD performance, or publication readiness.
