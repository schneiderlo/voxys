# LDraw foliage

Plant Leaves 6 x 5, part 2417, by James Jessiman, with the LDraw contributors
listed in the unmodified source headers and `ldraw-manifest.json`.

Source: https://library.ldraw.org/parts/2242
Library: https://www.ldraw.org/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
See `ldraw/CAlicense4.txt` and `ldraw/CAreadme.txt`.

The near forest models incorporate this part at one unit per stud, rotated and
recoloured on original Voxys trunks and crown volumes. Edge lines are omitted;
vertices are welded and faces triangulated for rendering. The medium and horizon
meshes use original simplified crown volumes. All downloaded DAT files are
retained unmodified; hashes are pinned in the manifest.
